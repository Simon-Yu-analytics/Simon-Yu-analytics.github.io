About my work

Connecting models
to their context.

My portfolio brings together machine learning, econometrics, and independent analytics projects.

Coursework at the University of Washington and self-directed projects have given me opportunities to work with retail time series, experimental data, consumer-operations workflows, and state-level economic indicators.

I focus on how a result is evaluated and what it can support: a competition score, an estimate from retained experimental cases, or a decision under explicit assumptions. Each case study includes the limits of its evidence.

Modeling & inference

Time-series forecasting, gradient boosting, model ensembles, regression, robust inference

Data & delivery

Python, SQL, R, Stata, reproducible pipelines, dashboards, clear research communication
